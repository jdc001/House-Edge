# House Edge

A Las Vegas casino tycoon game for iPhone, iPad and the web.

It's 1988. You're a junior associate at a New York merchant bank, sent west with $10 million of the firm's money and a $75 million credit line. Buy tired motels and empty desert, build themed resorts, bring in investors, take stakes in your rivals and take them over. Keep the Gaming Commission, the county and the Syndicate on side. The partners review you every two years. Keep making money and you keep your job.

## Play

Open the GitHub Pages site in Safari, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** to play it full screen like an app. Your career saves automatically on the device.

## Features

- A 3D night-time Strip you can pan, zoom and orbit, drawn with a small built-in canvas renderer (no libraries, no network needed after load)
- Quarterly turns from 1988 to 2047, with economic cycles loosely based on real Las Vegas history
- Five guest types with different tastes for themes, star ratings, prices, amenities and casino games
- Ownership stakes, control fights with four rival companies, outside investors with moods, bank credit and margin calls
- Resort designer: 10 themes, 9 amenities, casino floor size, game mix and table games
- Gaming licenses, regulator heat, county permits, the mob, unions, whales, headliners and board reviews

All names, properties and companies are fictional.
